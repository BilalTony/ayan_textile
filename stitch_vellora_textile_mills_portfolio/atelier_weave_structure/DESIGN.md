## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges industrial textile manufacturing with architectural high-fashion authority. The target audience comprises commercial architects, interior specifiers, luxury hospitality procurement leads, and industrial furniture designers. The emotional atmosphere must convey tactile permanence, uncompromising technical precision, disciplined luxury, and material depth.

The design movement is **Editorial Minimalism infused with Tactile Modernism**. It eschews superficial digital decor, opting for generous linen-inspired negative space, deliberate asymmetric editorial layouts, hairline border divisions reminiscent of loom warp and weft lines, and meticulous typographic hierarchies. UI components mimic architectural sample boards and textile swatch binders: clean, grounded, structural, and unyielding.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from raw fibers, mineral dyes, and architectural loom materials:

- **Primary (`#1A1E24`)**: Deep ink slate. Used for structural typography, high-priority interactive states, hairline structure rules, and technical data headers.
- **Secondary (`#C05638`)**: Rich terracotta clay. Used sparingly for curated accents, status indicators, active swatch toggles, and targeted call-to-actions.
- **Tertiary (`#2C4A3E`)**: Forest spruce. Used for sustainability badges, environmental certification stamps, natural fiber specifications, and subtle contrast blocks.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF9F5` / `#F4EFEA`)**: Warm raw ivory and woven linen cream. Serves as the primary canvas, providing optical warmth superior to stark sterile digital white.
- **Accent Muted Brass (`#C5A880`)**: Reserved for luxury archive badging, tactile yarn specification markers, and swatch finish highlights.
- **Structural Border (`#E8E2D9`)**: Crisp, subdued hairline boundary color reflecting structural grid intersections without heavy visual clutter.

Maintain stark color discipline: interactive surfaces should remain predominantly anchored in ink slate, linen cream, and crisp borders, letting photographic textile assets command the color field.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dialogue between European editorial literary prestige and technical industrial specification:

- **Playfair Display** introduces romantic, high-contrast serif grandeur for editorial collection titles, artistic manifestos, and prominent narrative markers. Headlines should embrace generous vertical line-height to avoid serif collision.
- **Inter** provides neutral, crystal-clear readability for technical fabric descriptions, Martindale rub test specs, fire retardancy ratings, and acoustic coefficient readouts.
- **Space Grotesk** serves as the utilitarian loom index marker: uppercase metadata tags, batch IDs, yarn composition percentages, and sample ledger indexing. Always pair Space Grotesk labels with uppercase styling and wide letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an **asymmetric 12-column architectural grid**, referencing high-end Swiss and Scandinavian print volumes:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters and `4rem` (64px) outer margins. Key editorial spreads intentionally combine large 7-column imagery spans with 5-column technical specification cards or off-center editorial copy blocks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2.5rem` (40px) outer margins. Columns collapse to 4+4 balanced pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins. Asymmetric layouts collapse linearly, maintaining strict vertical baseline rhythm.

Section transitions utilize dramatic `5rem` (`space-2xl`) vertical breathing room to preserve high-fashion restraint and prevent visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional fuzzy drop shadows and glossy skeuomorphism. Spatial depth is communicated through **monastic tonal layering and physical structural borders**:

1. **Zero-Elevation Base**: The ground canvas sits on `#FBF9F5`. Embedded content modules sit on `#F4EFEA`.
2. **Hairline Seams**: Visual containment is achieved via 1px crisp rules (`#E8E2D9`) echoing textile edge seams. No card carries floating ambient drop shadows under resting conditions.
3. **Specimen Lift (Hover Only)**: On direct interaction (such as hovering a textile sample or physical drape card), apply an architectonic, crisp offset shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 30, 36, 0.08)`.
4. **Modal Overlays & Swatch Trays**: Swatch binders and technical spec flyouts render with a semi-opaque backdrop blur of `#1A1E24` at 30% opacity, paired with an interior panel surface of `#FBF9F5` and a crisp 1px `#E8E2D9` border.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **strict, sharp architectural profile (`roundedness: 0`)**. 

Every interactive button, swatch card, modal frame, input box, and image container features sharp 90-degree corners (`0px` border-radius). This mirrors precision textile cutting mats, bolt edges, architectural blueprints, and high-end sample swatches. Curvature is strictly reserved for the natural drape of organic textiles shown inside uncropped photographs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#1A1E24` background, `#FBF9F5` text, 0px corner radius. Padding: `1rem 2rem`. Font: Space Grotesk, uppercase, tracking `0.06em`. Hover state: `#C05638` background with seamless 200ms ease transition.
- **Secondary (Technical Outline)**: 1px border `#1A1E24`, transparent background, `#1A1E24` text. Hover state: `#1A1E24` background with `#FBF9F5` text.
- **Tertiary (Editorial Link)**: Text-only in `#1A1E24`, underlined with an offset 1px hairline border `#C05638`. Hover state: underline shifts to 2px solid `#C05638`.

### Swatches & Material Cards
- Bound by a 1px `#E8E2D9` solid border on `#FBF9F5`.
- Features high-resolution macro photography with a fixed aspect ratio (3:4 or 1:1).
- Metadata label at bottom features fabric composition, weave type, and Martindale test cycles in Space Grotesk `label-sm`.
- Swatch selection triggers a 2px inner border of `#C05638` with a tiny terracotta triangular index tag at the top right.

### Input Fields
- Underline-only or structural boxed field: 1px bottom border in `#1A1E24` (or 1px bounding box in `#E8E2D9`).
- Background: transparent or `#F4EFEA`.
- Placeholder: `#1A1E24` at 40% opacity.
- Focus state: crisp 1px `#C05638` border, zero outer glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp 0px squares for both checkboxes and radio selectors.
- Checked checkbox: `#1A1E24` fill with a sharp white hairline check mark.
- Selected radio: `#1A1E24` outer border containing an inner `#C05638` sharp square inset.

### Chips & Technical Badges
- 1px border in `#E8E2D9`, background `#F4EFEA`. Space Grotesk `label-sm`, uppercase.
- Sustainable certification chips (e.g., Cradle to Cradle, OEKO-TEX) feature a subtle `#2C4A3E` left border accent (3px solid).

### Specimen Table / Spec Sheet
- Monospaced and tabular layout for warp/weft tensile strength, acoustic NRC ratings, and lightfastness.
- Alternating horizontal rules with 1px `#E8E2D9`; columns aligned with Space Grotesk headers and Inter data cells.
