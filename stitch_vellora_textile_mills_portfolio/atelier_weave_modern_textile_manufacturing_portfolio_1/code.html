Heritage & Outlook

Built on Craft. Growing with the World.

Founded in 1978 as a family-run shuttle-loom workshop, Atelier Weave has matured into a premier global textile partner for forward-thinking architects, furniture houses, and luxury hospitality chains.

1978 Loom House I
Mill Foundation

Began with 8 restored wooden mechanical looms weaving heavyweight cotton duck for industrial storage.

1996 Industrial Expansion
Dornier Modernization

Transitioned to high-speed electronic rapier looms, launching our dedicated contract upholstery line.

2012 Sustainability Milestone
Circular Weaving Initiative

Attained OEKO-TEX 100 and Global Recycled Standard accreditation; introduced 100% closed-loop post-industrial scrap reclaim.

2026 The Future
Net-Zero Bio-Filaments

Commercializing algae-dyed regenerative bast fibres engineered for Cradle-to-Cradle Gold architectural certification.
